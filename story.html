STORY SUMMARY

(spoiler free)


Deltarune starts with a basic, but very cool, character customization screen. A godly voice guides you as you create your perfect vessel, with likes, dislikes, style, and name. But when you complete it, the ominous voice says:
“
THANK YOU
FOR YOUR TIME.
YOUR ANSWERS
YOUR WONDERFUL CREATION

Will now be discarded.
No one can choose who they are in this world.
Your
name
is
…
*Kris…!
”
You, the player, now play as Kris, the lone human in a small town of “Monsters” going through school. But after a series of events, you end up falling down a dark, dark pit inside a storage closet with the school bully Susie. There, you end up in what’s called a “Dark World”; a world created by an immense amount of will powered into the stabbing of the earth, creating a sort of pocket dimension where objects become beings known as “Darkners”. From there, you meet your guide for the game, Ralsei, who tells you of the prophecy, and the 3 Delta-Warriors who are the heroes that will save the world from “The Roaring”, and the villainous Knight. The Roaring being an apocalyptic event where too many “dark fountains” are created (the sort of gateway between the light and dark worlds), causing the sky to turn black and Titans to rise from the depths. He is sure you, Susie, and himself are the 3 heroes of this story, “A HUMAN, A MONSTER, And a PRINCE FROM THE DARK.”

From there, you play through the game, fighting threats with an RPG battle system, discovering new items, and recruiting enemies. The fighting system is actually really fun too. You avoid bullets and attacks in a box as a heart, while taking turns using moves and actions with your party. Recruits join you in Castle Town, the dark world town made to store recruits; the home of Ralsei and someone else as we later learn…

But, there’s much more story than this typical action-fantasy adventure. A missing girl, conspiracy, betrayal, secret enemies, the fight for freedom of choice where they don’t matter, mysteries, etc. However, the biggest of these reveals for the story going forward happens at the end of chapter 1. Your player character, Kris, is returned back to their room to sleep, only to then viciously rip out their heart (the same one we control when fighting), and throw it into a cage, brandishing a knife soon after. You are NOT Kris. You are the PLAYER. YOU, are the SOUL.
