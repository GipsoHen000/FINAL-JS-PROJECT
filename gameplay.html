GAMEPLAY


This game is like a mix of an RPG, a visual novel, and an (older) Pokemon game. The main gameplay is split up between traveling the map, which can very between platforming, open movement, and select paths, and fighting enemies.

With movement, you can go basically anywhere at isn't obstructed, up, left, right, or down. You can go to the edge of a screen to go to the next, save with 4-pointed stars around each map, and use special doors with flames under them to fast-travel to other doors within the same dark world. But you can also do lots of other unique things throughout the game that aren't part of the base gameplay such as moving objects, playing intruments, platforming, avoiding attacks (outside of a battle), and so much more. There are also shops throughout the game, but they don't really fit anywhere else, so I'm putting them here.

However, the story is mainly conveyed through text boxes on the bottom of the screen. Like this:


Here, you can see the text beside a " * " which indicates a new line. As well as, with light world characters, faces to go along side the dialogue! You can also select dialogue options in this box using your soul, even if sometimes they don't impact the game. But sometimes, they DO impact the game. They can REALLY impact the game. Some characters also have special text-quirks like "TALKING [like and subscrib!!!1!] THIS", "Or... This...", "oR lOiKe DiS!! lOl!". Yeah, some of them aren't that good, but some are still quite cool tho.

The battling mechanic is actual quite complex while still being fun! You can encounter enemies, whether it's at random or not, and choose between 3 main options while dodging attacks. But first, you play as the soul when fighting, usually trapped in a green box where you avoid attacks like projectiles, bombs, and basically any other attack you can think of. But you also have the mechanic of TP. TP is like a near miss system, where if you get close enough to an attack without taking damage, you gain some. TP can be used to access certain attacks and other moves between turns, as well as some fight-specific other things sometimes.
But from there, you can choose between 3 main options between turns for each party member. The first is to attack, which obviously attacks the enemy. The second is to use items and/or heal, which is either you choosing to use an item on a party member (likely to heal them or gain TP), or using a spell to heal them with Ralsei. Then finally, you can recruit, which after dealing enough damage to an enemy, you can choose to spare it and take it back to Castle Town. Other miscellaneous moves include flirt, which makes an enemy easier to recruit, Flee, to avoid the fight, choosing to look at the enemies stats such as health, name, and description, and other S and R Actions for Susie and Ralsei. Those can change and vary with some specific fights, as well as attacks and some TP moves as mentioned earlier.
